Season Overview

The 19th Edition Burns Bright

The 2026 Indian Premier League kicked off on March 28 at the iconic M. Chinnaswamy Stadium in Bengaluru — a venue returning to cricket for the first time since a tragic stampede incident — with defending champions Royal Challengers Bengaluru hosting Sunrisers Hyderabad in a mouth-watering opener. Nearly two months later, the tournament is barrelling toward a nerve-wracking finish.

This season marks a significant expansion, with the tournament stretched to 84 league matches — up from previous editions — spread across 12 cities in 12 major venues. The format remains the same: 10 franchises divided into two groups, fighting for four precious playoff slots, with the top teams battling it out in Qualifiers before the grand final on June 1.

RCB, Gujarat Titans and Sunrisers Hyderabad have already sealed their playoff berths — but that fourth spot is still anyone's game with the final round of fixtures on May 24.

The race for that final playoff spot is currently a tense, four-way scramble involving Rajasthan Royals, Punjab Kings, Kolkata Knight Riders, and Delhi Capitals — each knowing that one slip means elimination. Lucknow Super Giants and Mumbai Indians have both been mathematically eliminated, while Chennai Super Kings' campaign fizzled out far earlier than their fans had hoped.
