# Aganox LMS — User Manual

**Version:** 1.0  
**Audience:** Employees and managers using the leave system

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the URL provided by your administrator (e.g. `https://localhost:8443`).
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. If prompted, set a new password before continuing.

Forgot your password? Click **Forgot password** and follow the email instructions (contact HR if you don’t receive email).

---

## 2. Dashboard

After login you see a role-based dashboard:

- **Leave balance** — remaining days by leave type
- **Recent requests** — status of your applications
- **Quick actions** — apply for leave, view history

Managers additionally see team on-leave today and pending team approvals.

---

## 3. Applying for leave

1. Go to **Leave → Apply** (or **New request**).
2. Select **leave type**, **from/to dates**, and optional half-day.
3. Add **remarks** if needed.
4. **Save as draft** to finish later, or **Submit** to send for approval.

### Tips

- The system blocks **overlapping** leave and insufficient balance (unless HR allows negative balance for that type).
- **Weekends and holidays** may be excluded based on company policy — the day count reflects this.
- You can **cancel** pending requests; approved leave may require HR to adjust.

---

## 4. Tracking your requests

**Leave → My requests** lists all your applications.

Open a request to see:

- Status (Draft, Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled)
- Approval steps and who acted
- Comments from approvers

---

## 5. Leave balances

View current-year balances on the dashboard or employee profile.

Balances update when leave is **finally approved**. If HR makes a manual adjustment, it appears in your balance history.

---

## 6. For managers

### Approvals inbox

**Approvals → Inbox** shows leave waiting for your action.

- **Approve** — optional comment, forwards to next step or finalizes (if last step)
- **Reject** — comment required; employee is notified

### Team visibility

Dashboard and reports show who is on leave today and team trends (based on your permissions).

---

## 7. Changing your password

**Profile / Change password** (or forced redirect after first login):

1. Current password
2. New password (must meet complexity rules)
3. Confirm new password

---

## 8. Status glossary

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Draft | Not yet submitted |
| Pending | Awaiting approver action |
| Approved | Fully approved; balance deducted |
| Rejected | Declined by an approver |
| Cancelled | Withdrawn by you or HR |

---

## 9. Getting help

- Leave policy questions → your HR department
- Login or system errors → IT support (see internal contact from your admin)

Administrators: see [Administrator Guide](../admin-guide/README.md).
